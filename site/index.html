Questions camps ask.

Do we need to install anything?

No. Headcount runs in a web browser on a computer you already have, such as the one at your front desk.

Does it track campers?

No. Headcount is for staff only.

Why not a paper sheet or a Google Form?

A paper sheet cannot tell you who is late, and nobody can read it from across camp. Headcount shows everyone on one screen and alerts the director when someone is overdue.

Where does our data live, and who can see it?

In a Google Sheet in your own Google account. Each camp has its own, and nothing is shared between camps. Headcount reaches your Sheet through an account you can remove at any time. We store names, reasons, times, and each staff code in scrambled form, so nobody reading the Sheet can see a code. We do not store campers, home addresses or phone numbers.

What if you are unavailable?

I run Headcount myself, and I get an alert on my phone if your board has a problem, so I often know before you do. You always keep your records, because they live in your own Google Sheet. If I ever stopped, you could remove my access and keep every record.

What if the internet goes down?

If Google is slow or unreachable, Headcount keeps trying and saves each sign-out the moment it can. If the kiosk computer itself loses its internet connection, it cannot load the board until it reconnects.

Is it a safety system?

No. It shows where your staff say they are, and who is overdue. It does not replace your emergency procedures.
